Check your browser’s voice

This plays a short fixed phrase directly through your browser, without the reading app’s playback controls. It does not use your passage.

Choose a voice, then test. Listen for the spoken phrase.

If both tests fail on macOS, fully quit Chrome with ⌘Q and reopen it. If they still fail, compare this page in Safari and test a voice in macOS Accessibility → Read & Speak (called Spoken Content on older versions).

Diagnostic report

  
  
The report contains browser/version information, available voice names, and speech events. It contains no pasted Chinese or English text.
